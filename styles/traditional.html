About Traditional Tattoos

Traditional tattoos, also known as American Traditional or Old School, feature bold black outlines, a limited but vibrant color palette, and iconic imagery.

This timeless style emerged from sailor culture and has become one of the most recognizable tattoo styles worldwide. Traditional tattoos are known for their longevity - the bold lines and solid colors age exceptionally well over time.

Classic motifs include anchors, roses, eagles, skulls, pin-up girls, daggers, and nautical themes. Each design carries rich symbolism and heritage.
