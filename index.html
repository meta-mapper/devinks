About Us

A Studio Built on Trust & Transparency

Dev Inks Tattoo Factory is built around one simple belief: getting a tattoo should feel clear, comfortable, and honest.

We exist for people who want meaningful tattoos without intimidation, confusion, or unnecessary pricing pressure. Our approach is client-led. Every tattoo starts with the individual, their reference, and their intent.

We focus on listening, explaining the process clearly, and executing clean work with care and consistency. There is no pressure to fit a style, no judgement for asking questions, and no attitude around experience or budget.

FAQ

Common Questions

Pain varies by person and placement. Most describe it as a scratching sensation. We use techniques to minimize discomfort and can take breaks as needed. First-timers are often surprised it's more bearable than expected!

Get a good night's sleep, eat a proper meal before your appointment, stay hydrated, avoid alcohol 24 hours before, and wear comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the tattoo area.

Surface healing takes 2-3 weeks, while complete healing takes 2-3 months. We provide detailed aftercare instructions to ensure optimal healing and color retention.
